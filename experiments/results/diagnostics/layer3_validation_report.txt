Theoretical Derivation Notes:
  - The multi-round dropout state machine forms a discrete-time renewal process.
  - Steady-state probability of being in an offline episode of duration D rounds:
      P(offline) = (D * p_drop) / (1 + (D - 1) * p_drop)
  - Combined with independent packet loss for online clients, total absorption is:
      P(drop_total) = P(offline) + (1 - P(offline)) * packet_loss_rate
  - Urban Zambia: Theoretical 5.95% vs Empirical 5.60% (Δ = -0.35 pp).
  - Rural Zambia: Theoretical 31.26% vs Empirical 30.20% (Δ = -1.06 pp).
  - Severe Disruption: Theoretical steady-state 69.41% vs Empirical 20-round transient 59.80% (Δ = -9.61 pp,
    attributable to all clients starting in the online state at round 0 before reaching ergodicity).
